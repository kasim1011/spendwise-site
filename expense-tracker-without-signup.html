No account

An expense tracker without signup, account or email

Ask why an expense tracker needs your email address and the honest answer is usually "so we can sync it, bill you, or market to you". Spendwise does none of those, so it asks for nothing. This page explains what that looks like in practice.

Updated: 20 October 2026  ·  First run: language and currency only  ·  Data: on your phone

What first launch actually shows

Open Spendwise for the first time and it asks two things: which language you read, and which currency your money is in. The currency list is the full ISO set, formatting only — 156 codes, with correct decimals for yen and dinars. Both choices are changeable later in Settings.

Then you are in the app. No email field, no password, no "verify your address", no marketing consent checkbox, no login. The categories are already seeded, the dashboard is ready, and the first expense can be logged within a minute of installing.

Three things that change without an account

Nobody can lock you out of your own history

There is no password to reset, no session to expire and no server that can decide you are no longer welcome. The ledger is in the app's private storage on your phone, so the phone's own lock screen is the only gate — and if you want a second gate, App Lock asks for your device PIN, pattern or biometrics before Spendwise opens.

The company has no copy of your spending

Spendwise has no backend that stores transactions. There is no profile describing your groceries, no customer record to breach, and nothing to hand over if someone asks. The data route in and out of the app is the one you choose: a backup file or a report export.

The trade: the backup is entirely yours

A cloud account doubles as an off-device backup. Here, uninstalling the app deletes the ledger. Spendwise's answer is one file: Settings → Backup & Restore → Create backup file writes every transaction, category, budget, person, group and receipt attachment into a single .spendwise file. Restore it on a new phone and you are back. Keep it somewhere safe, and remember it is not encrypted.

Why the other apps ask for an account

None of this is a trick on their part. An account is how a tracker offers:

  • Sync — the same ledger on a phone, a tablet and a web app.
  • Bank imports — transactions pulled in automatically.
  • Billing — a subscription needs a person to attach it to.
  • Retention — an email address is a way to win you back after you stop opening the app.

Spendwise offers none of the first three, so the fourth has nothing to attach to. If you want bank sync and multi-device ledgers, an account is a fair price — that is exactly what the Spendee comparison says. If you are here because you do not want another login, the rest of this page is the part that matters.

What pays for it instead

Spendwise is free with no in-app purchases, and it does not sell data. The revenue is a single non-personalised ad on the transaction confirmation screen — the one screen where a task is already finished and the middle of the page is empty. In the EEA and UK a consent prompt governs it; where consent is refused, the ad does not show.

Anonymous screen analytics are the other network feature: which screens get used, keyed to route names, with no user id, no email, no advertising identifier and no tracking ID. They are on by default and can be switched off in Settings, after which nothing is sent. The privacy page lists the details.

Who this suits, and who it does not

It suits people who want a fast personal ledger on one phone, who would rather type an expense than hand over an inbox, and who are willing to keep a backup file. It also suits anyone who has abandoned three cloud trackers because the login friction beat the benefit.

It does not suit a household that wants each member's phone to update the same ledger live, or anyone who refuses to tap in expenses and needs bank imports. A synced app is the right answer there, and this site will not pretend otherwise.

Frequently asked questions

Does Spendwise really need no email address?

Correct. There is no sign-up screen. First launch asks for a language and a currency, both changeable later in Settings, and that is the whole setup.

What happens if I lose my phone?

The ledger on that phone is gone unless you made a backup file. There is no account to recover it from, which is the same reason nobody else can read it.

Can I use one Spendwise ledger on two phones?

Not at the same time. A backup file can be restored onto a second phone, but the two do not sync, and restoring replaces what is on that phone. See how the offline ledger works.

How does Spendwise make money without accounts?

One non-personalised ad on the transaction confirmation screen. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no data sold; anonymous screen analytics can be switched off in Settings.

How do I delete everything Spendwise stored?

Uninstalling the app removes its private storage, or clear the app's data in Android settings. Spendwise never had a copy to delete on a server.
